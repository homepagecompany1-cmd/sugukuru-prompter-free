Loading your script…

On: script appears → voice reads the question → beep → recording starts. Off: script appears → beep → recording starts.

  1. Stand your phone up (a tripod helps) with the front camera facing you
  2. When you hear the beep, read the script on screen
  3. When you finish, say “next” (or tap Next)
  4. At the end, save all clips at once

Your videos stay on this phone; this page uploads nothing. If voice “next” is on, your browser’s speech service (e.g., Apple or Google) may process your audio — turn it off and tap Next if you prefer. Your script lives after the “#” in the URL and never reaches our server, but anyone with the link can read it.
